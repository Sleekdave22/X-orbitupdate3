# Timed Vibes and cross-module navigation

Extends the verified version 31 interface without changing messages, calls, matches, finances or GuardianLab policy.

- Authenticated navigation summary supplies unread-message totals and permitted active Vibe references. Hidden, deleted and blocked messages are excluded. Selected Private Vibe audiences remain enforced.
- Chat badge and profile Vibe markers use that summary in browser and Android. Play and Market gain links to the other existing modules.
- Photos play for ten visible seconds and videos for twenty, with increasing progress. Background time, video pauses, replying and Private Vibe shielding do not consume the viewing window.
- Downward dragging follows the finger and a short flick or sufficient partial drag returns to the list. Camera beside My Vibe offers photo, twenty-second video and the existing file picker.
- Existing evidence camera defaults remain sixty seconds; Vibe opts into a shorter limit and neutral wording. Upload limits and existing Vibe expiry settings are preserved.

Validation is recorded in the final Marketplace extension report. These changes have not yet been deployed at this intermediate checkpoint.
